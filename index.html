What we do.

We give old electronics a better next chapter.

Electronics recycling, made simple.

At Stem Tech Pickup, we recycle your old or broken electronics. We collect them directly from your doorstep, so there is nothing to drop off and nowhere to go.

We accept nearly everything.

Laptops, phones, tablets, monitors, cables, and more. Working or broken, complete or in pieces. Even individual parts are welcome.

Our mission.

Electronic waste is a growing global challenge. We are working to reduce it by offering a donation service for old, used, broken, or simply unwanted electronics.

Every item we collect is repurposed where possible, or directed to responsible recycling channels.

Completely free.

There is no charge to schedule a pick up, and you are not paid for your items. No money changes hands in either direction.

It is a straightforward donation, designed to help you clear out technology you no longer need without the guilt of throwing it away.

How it works.

  1. 1
    Schedule a pick up.Choose a date and a two-hour window that suits you.
  2. 2
    Prepare your items.Place your electronics in a box, bag, or bin and add a note that says STEM TECH PICKUP.
  3. 3
    Leave them on your doorstep.Set everything out before your pick up time.
  4. 4
    We take it from here.We collect your items and route them to reuse or responsible recycling.

Independently founded.

Stem Tech Pickup is an independent business started by a student working their way through college. It is run with care, one pick up at a time.
